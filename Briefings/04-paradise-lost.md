# 04 Paradise Lost

Legend of the Invincibles, Part I, NORMAL. Read off the live turn 1 board (`LotI1-Paradise Lost-Auto-Save1`), the scenario file and the map.

## The shape of it

Three orc camps, two allies who are already losing, and twenty-five turns to kill all three orc leaders. It is the first scenario in this campaign with no free phase: everything is hostile from turn 1, all three enemy sides start funded, and the clock is short for the distances involved.

The structural fact that makes it manageable: **the three camps are nowhere near each other and one of them is right on top of you.** Grub Drung sits thirteen movement south of your keep with **one movement point**, so he cannot run, cannot reposition, and cannot come to you. The other two are forty-five and forty-six away, in opposite corners.

## The one number that drives every decision

**Thirty-three and thirty-four.** That is the movement cost from Grub Drung's keep at (23,13) to Brag in the south-east and Vrongromor in the south-west. They are equidistant from the centre and roughly seventy apart from each other.

You cannot do them in sequence. Killing Grub Drung by turn six leaves nineteen turns, and one camp costs six turns of travel plus five to break. Two camps in sequence costs thirty. **So the army splits after the first kill, and the whole plan is really about being big enough to split.**

## Lethalia is much safer than the objective makes her look

Two things protect her, and neither is visible in game.

**The orcs hit her 10% of the time.** All three enemy sides have their chance to hit her forced down to one in ten, for the whole scenario. She is 50 hit points of Enchantress standing behind a 90% miss rate.

**There are two safety nets, both before turn 8.** If the Woses drop below three units, five more appear across the west. If the elves lose every unit except Lethalia herself, six high-level elves appear around her, an Elvish Lord, a Hero, a Marksman and three Avengers, plus 150 gold.

So do not panic-march south-west to rescue her. She is 33 movement away, the trip costs four turns at Delenia's speed and six at everyone else's, and the game has already decided she is not going to die easily.

## The ground

46 wide by 31 tall, and it is forest almost everywhere, cut by rivers. Your elusivefoot core, Delenia, the three Rogues, the Thieves and the Footpads, is hit **30% of the time in forest** and 40% on flat. Stay in the trees and stay out of the water.

**Six keeps.** Yours at (22,3). Grub Drung's at (23,13) inside a large castle. Lethalia's at (13,23). Vrongromor's at (4,26) and Brag's at (36,26). And a **free unclaimed keep at (12,18)**, which sits between you and Lethalia.

**Grub Drung's castle is the prize, not just his head.** Taking (23,13) gives you a recruiting base in the centre of the map, thirty-three or thirty-four from either remaining camp. Every reinforcement you buy after that starts from there instead of walking down from (22,3).

**38 villages.** They are spread evenly and both you and the orcs will be taking them all scenario. This is not scenario 3: nobody hands you the economy.

## What there is to loot

Ordinary kills are **5% item and 7% gem**, which is barely worth counting. Do not grind orcs for loot in this scenario; you will be killing dozens and expecting two or three drops from the lot.

**The boss drops are guaranteed.** All three orc leaders drop an item when they die, and you have to kill all three to win anyway. That is the loot: three certain items, and they arrive as a consequence of the objective rather than as a detour.

## Who to bring

Nineteen units, and the composition has shifted hard toward chaotic outlaws: Delenia, three level 2 Rogues, three Thieves, three Poachers, four Thugs, a Footpad, plus Deoran and Hallaent as the only lawful fighters and Mario still at 43 of 72.

Bring nearly all of it. You are splitting into two columns and each needs a spine.

**Mario stays on the bench again.** The reasoning has not changed: his advancement is a plain level 1 unit, because a level 1 Sergeant's leadership is worth zero against a roster with no level 0 units in it, and this scenario has no safe corner for an 18 hit point unit.

## Opening budget

371 gold, and only **six castle hexes**, so you can put six units on the board per turn no matter how rich you are. Deployment takes three turns whatever you do.

- **Turn 1**: recall the three Rogues, Huwyn, Ylla and Teowell (60), plus Deoran and two Thugs (60). That is six on the board and 120 spent, leaving 251.
- **Turn 2**: recall the remaining Thieves, Poachers and Thugs, six more, another 120. Leaving about 131.
- **Turn 3 onward**: recruit fresh Thugs and Poachers at 13 and 14 into the free slots as the first wave marches out, and keep buying from Grub Drung's keep once it is yours.

Recall over recruit here, unusually, because your bench is deep and carries real experience: Deoran at 20 of 44, Yddin at 16, Giraryn at 13, Hallaent at 12, two Thieves at 11. Those are units two or three kills from levelling, and a fresh Thug at 13 gold is not.

Watch upkeep. Nineteen fielded units of mixed level is roughly twenty a turn, against 38 contested villages and two base income. You will be net negative until you hold a dozen villages.

## The plan

**Turns 1 to 3, deploy and march.** Equip first, then fill the castle each turn and send each wave south as it lands. Delenia scouts ahead at movement 9 but does not engage alone.

**Turns 3 to 6, kill Grub Drung.** He is thirteen movement away and has one movement point, so he cannot avoid you, cannot retreat, and cannot reinforce the other camps. He will have spent his 250 gold on defenders inside a large castle, so expect a real fight on castle hexes where his units are hit 40% of the time. Land the attack on turn 6, which is Morning.

**Turn 6 to 7, take the keep.** Put Efraim on (23,13) and recruit the second army there. This is the single most valuable move in the scenario: it converts a forty-six hex march into a thirty-three hex one for everything you buy afterwards.

**Turns 7 to 13, split.** West column with Delenia toward Vrongromor at (4,26), thirty-four away, picking up Lethalia's elves and the Woses as it goes. East column toward Brag at (36,26), thirty-three away. Brag is the softer target at 56 hit points and an Orcish Slurbow is a ranged unit, so the east column wants the Poachers and Deoran to answer him at range.

**Turns 13 to 22, break both camps.** Each column has to crack a castle. Use the daylight turns, 13, 18, 19 and 24, for the assaults.

**Turns 22 to 25, slack.** If one camp is going faster than the other, send its survivors sideways rather than home.

## What will actually hurt you

**1. The split.** Two columns of nine or ten against a camp each is thin, and a column that stalls cannot be reinforced from the other side of the map. The mitigation is Grub Drung's keep: hold it, keep Efraim there or near, and feed both columns from the centre.

**2. Sixty-two income a turn.** The orcs earn more per turn than you do and there are three of them. Every turn you spend not killing a leader is roughly two more orcs on the board. Killing Grub Drung early removes 22 of that permanently.

**3. Orcish Crossbowmen and Brag himself.** New at this difficulty, and a Slurbow is a heavy ranged attacker. Your Thieves at 23 to 29 hit points do not want to be the ones standing in front of him.

**4. Lethalia, but only through carelessness.** She is at 10% to be hit and double-backstopped. The realistic way to lose her is your own column pulling her defenders away, not the orcs killing her.

**5. Efraim and Delenia, as always.** Both are level 3 now, 57 and 62 hit points, and both are carrying the items you cannot replace.

## If it goes wrong

There is no cheap win here: the objective is all three leaders, so there is no early cash-out. The fallback is narrower. If one column stalls, consolidate both on the nearer camp, kill that leader, and accept losing the scenario on the clock rather than losing Efraim, Delenia or the roster. A defeat costs one replay; nine dead veterans, which is what scenario 3 cost, compounds for the rest of the campaign.

## Still unverified

- **Whether a Thug can wear the Heathenbasher.** Equip it and read the attack line. If the club does not change, the mace is dead weight until a mace user appears.
- **Whether anything on this map resists lightning less than blade.** If something does, Stormrend becomes interesting; against orcs it does not.
- **How aggressively the three orc sides fight each other's neighbours.** Sides 3, 4 and 5 all share the Evil team, so they will not fight each other, but how much of their output goes at the elves and Woses rather than at you decides how much of the 620 gold you actually have to face.
- **Whether Grub Drung's one movement point is a guardian flag or just a slow unit.** Either way he is not leaving his keep, but it changes whether he steps onto an adjacent castle hex to fight.
