# 02 The Assassination

Legend of the Invincibles, Part I, NORMAL. Written from the board as it stands on turn 3 of 17.

## What you are walking into

Redain sits in a locked chamber at the far west end of his own castle with nine elite guards between you and him. You have seventeen turns, and running out of them is a loss, not a draw. So is losing Efraim. So is losing Delenia.

But the scenario is not the brawl it looks like. Read the next section before you move anything.

## The garrison is not hostile yet

Redain's men are on your side until you walk into his chamber. Not neutral: actually allied. You cannot attack them, they cannot attack you, and you can walk straight through them as if they were your own units.

Efraim says it in the opening line and it is literally true: "Do NOT make any sudden movements towards him until we are all in position."

The truce breaks the instant one of your units steps on **(2,5), (3,6), (3,7), (2,7), (1,7) or (1,6)**. Those six hexes are Redain's chamber. Until you touch one of them you have a free deployment phase: explore the whole castle, take every village, walk your army to whatever hex you like, and start the war on the turn you choose.

If you want to check this in game, look at the minimap colours, or run `bun run map` and read the last line. It says `hostile sides: none`.

One caution: it is not confirmed whether stepping *through* one of those six hexes on the way somewhere else also breaks the truce. Route around them until you mean it.

All of them are lawful except Lyryn, who is neutral.

## It is night, and it stays night

The whole scenario is fixed at Second Watch. There is no dawn coming. For all seventeen turns:

- **Your chaotic units swing at 125%.** Delenia, both Rogues, every Thief, the Footpad, every Thug.
- **The whole garrison swings at 75%.** Except Lyryn, who does not care.
- **Efraim is neutral, but his weapons are not.** His Sword of Krux is a lawful weapon and fights at 75%. His shadow wave is chaotic and fights at 125%. At night he should be casting, not swinging, unless the sword's raw numbers win anyway.

That fifty point gap is the biggest edge you have in this scenario, bigger than any item. It is also why the recruit list is what it is.

## What you can buy

The Fencers and the rest of the regular army refuse to march against their own lord. What you get instead is Delenia's friends: **Footpad, Rogue, Thief and Thug**, every one of them chaotic.

Thief 13, Thug 13, Footpad 14, Rogue 25, recall 20.

## The ground

**There is one door.** The wall across the middle of the map has exactly one gap, at **(14,10)**. Everything that comes at you comes through that hex. Inside it opens onto (13,9), (14,9) and (15,9); outside, onto (14,11).

**Behind the door is a killing floor.** Two keeps sit at (14,11) and (14,12) with castle hexes around them. Anything standing on (14,11) can only be reached from the door itself, one attacker at a time. Anything that pushes on to (14,12) can be surrounded by five of your units. This is where you want the fight, not inside the castle.

**Redain's chamber has one blind spot.** He has five open hexes around him, and four of them wake him up. **(3,5) does not.** You can put a unit on his shoulder and he will keep talking to you as if nothing is happening.

**Backstab needs the hex directly opposite.** Across Redain that means:

| Pair | Can you use it |
|---|---|
| (2,5) and (2,7) | Yes, both empty |
| (3,5) and (1,6) | Only once Vugthynaent is off (1,6) |
| (3,6) and (1,5) | Never, (1,5) is solid rock |

**The chamber has one entrance,** through **(4,6)** and **(4,7)**. Tempting as it looks, do not try to hold that doorway: Redain and his bodyguard would be behind you, so you would be taking hits from five directions instead of two.

**Two guards trap themselves.** Buroc at (11,9) and Gloraevan at (17,9) sit in alcoves with a single way out. One unit on (12,9) or (16,9) corks each of them for the rest of the scenario. Only do it with a body you are willing to lose, because a corked Royal Guard can still swing at whoever is corking him.

**Every village is outside the castle:** (11,12), (17,12), (10,13), (17,13), (10,15), (5,16), (14,16) and (20,16). Eight of them, one gold each. They are your only healing and the only thing that cures poison. The garrison has none, which matters more than it sounds.

**There is a free treasure at (21,1)**, the far north-east corner, currently underneath Vinogyc. Grab it during the truce if he wanders off, otherwise take it after he dies.

## Your gear

Right now Efraim is wearing everything and Delenia is wearing almost nothing.

| Unit | Carrying |
|---|---|
| Efraim | Sword of Krux, Potion of Steel Skin, Leather Armour, Virtual Void |
| Delenia | Twinkling Sword |
| Everyone else | nothing at all |

Still in the bag: **Poison**, **Heathenbasher** (two of them), **Rherraent's Sword**, and **The Doctrine of Vindictiveness**.

You can open the bag any time. Shift+I on any of your units, any turn, anywhere on the map. There is no keep or village requirement, so there is no reason to be walking around with five items unused.

### Move Virtual Void to Delenia

This is the single biggest thing you can do this turn.

On Efraim, the helm turns a 7x4 blade sword into 11x4 cold, and then his own weapon's lawful penalty knocks it back to 8x4 at night. Nice, not decisive.

On Delenia, the same +4 lands on a sword that is *already* cold, so nothing is lost in the swap, and then it multiplies twice over. Base 8 becomes 12. Backstab doubles it to 24. Night takes it to **30 per strike**.

Against Redain's 63 hit points at 60% to hit, that changes what she needs:

| Delenia backstabbing Redain | Damage per strike | Chance she kills him alone |
|---|---|---|
| Sword only, as she is now | 20 | 13% |
| With Virtual Void | 30 | **47%** |

Put Huwyn on the opposite hex for another 25 or so and Redain dies on the turn you decide he dies.

### The rest of the bag

**Poison goes to Huwyn.** Three strikes at 60% means he lands at least one 94% of the time. Poison does 8 a turn and never kills on its own, it just walks a unit down to 1 hit point and leaves it there. An 82 hit point Royal Guard becomes something any Thief can finish for full experience, and since the garrison holds no villages, nothing they do will cure it. This is what makes grinding the guards safe instead of expensive.

**The Doctrine of Vindictiveness goes to Delenia.** It unlocks an advancement line, and she is the only unit within sight of one.

**Leather Armour stays on Efraim.** It cuts the blade and pierce damage he takes by about a fifth. Be clear about what it does not do: nothing at all against Lyryn's fire, which is the thing most likely to kill him.

**Leave both Heathenbashers in the bag.** They glow. Illuminates pushes every neighbouring hex from night toward day, which would strip the 125% off your own chaotic army and hand the garrison its own bonus back. They are a fine pair of maces for Aran on a daytime map. Not here.

**Leave Rherraent's Sword in the bag.** The only free sword slots belong to the three Fencers, and the Fencers are staying home. Give it to Aeraent next scenario.

**Do not put Virtual Void on anyone you plan to send at Lyryn.** She shrugs off half of all cold damage, which is why Delenia's 8 damage sword reads 5 against her. Huwyn's dagger is your answer to the Silver Mage and it needs to stay a blade.

## Who to bring

Bring the chaotic half. Leave the rest at home.

A lawful veteran here fights at three quarters strength for seventeen straight turns against 71 to 82 hit point heavies. That is not training a veteran, that is arranging a funeral.

**Field:** Delenia, Efraim, Huwyn, Ynoc, Addreorer, Leoraeran, Rorcyll, Yrreddyn, and Caeraeka off the recall list for 20.

**Bench:** Deoran and Evan (Bowmen), Meotharryn, Aeraent and Syry (Fencers), Hallaent (Spearman), Aran (Heavy Infantryman).

**Mario** is already out and he is lawful with 18 hit points, so he cannot fight here. He is loyal and sitting at 43 of 72 experience, so he is worth growing. Park him on a village behind the line and let him land the finishing blow on something already poisoned down to nothing.

On money: Delenia and Mario are loyal and cost no upkeep, Efraim is your leader and costs none either. The other eight cost 8 a turn against 8 villages, so you are breaking even. Every additional recruit costs you a gold a turn from here.

## The plan

Turn 3, 87 gold, nine units on the map, fourteen turns to work with.

**Turn 3, take the free actions.** Swap Virtual Void onto Delenia, Poison onto Huwyn, the book onto Delenia. Recall Caeraeka. Walk Efraim up to the keep at (14,12), which is closer to the fight and has more castle around it than his starting keep, and recruit two or three Thugs there. Send whoever is spare to sit on the villages you do not already hold.

**Turns 3 to 5, walk in.** Nothing can touch you, so move at full speed and split in two.

*The strike team* is Delenia, Huwyn, Ynoc and two Thieves. Through the door, west along the row at y=9, up through (12,8), (11,7), (10,7), then north-west by (8,7), (7,6), (7,5), (6,5) to the chamber doorway at (5,5) and (5,7). Delenia moves 9 and will arrive first. Do not let her arrive alone and tempt you into starting early.

*The anvil* is the Footpad, the Thugs, Mario and a Thief, holding the courtyard at (13,11), (14,12) and (15,11) and the two nearest villages. This is where the fight is supposed to happen.

**Starting the fight.** Delenia steps onto (3,6), the argument plays out, and then she keeps walking the same turn, straight back out east. From (4,7) that is one hex in and four hexes back to safety, well inside her nine. She is the right unit for the job for a reason the game hides from you: Redain's chance to hit her, and Efraim, is quietly forced down to 20% for the whole scenario. He is nearly harmless to those two specifically, and dangerous to everyone else.

**Turns 6 to 13, make them come to you.** Pull back to the courtyard and let them file out through (14,10) one at a time. Anything that steps out can be hit by three of your units; anything that pushes to (14,12) can be hit by five. Meanwhile Huwyn touches everything he can reach and walks away, and the poison collects the debt.

**Give the kills to the Thieves.** A dead Royal Guard is 24 experience and a Thief levels at 29, so a single kill almost turns a Thief into a Rogue. Delenia needs 113 for her next advancement and will not see it this scenario, so she should be the one doing damage, not the one landing the last blow. This is the point of the whole scenario. Nine elite kills at a 30% drop rate is the best farm you have had so far, and it is the exact opposite of what your instincts say in normal Wesnoth.

**Turns 13 to 16, finish it.** Walk Delenia and Huwyn back west, put them on (2,5) and (2,7), and kill Redain. Bring Efraim if it is safe: he is at 23 of 25 experience and one connecting blow on any of these guards levels him, which also heals him to full. Leave two turns of slack. The clock kills you if you get greedy.

## What will actually hurt you

**1. Lyryn.** 9x4 fire, magical, which means terrain does not help and armour does not either. Neutral, so the night does not slow her down. Against Efraim that is a **24% chance to kill him outright, per attack**, and Efraim dying ends the run. Keep him seven hexes clear of her until she is dead. The good news is she is the softest thing in the castle at 55 hit points, and Huwyn's backstab takes 27 of them in one go.

**2. Losing Delenia or Efraim to anything at all.** Two of your three loss conditions are single units. Neither should ever finish a turn next to more than one enemy.

**3. The clock.** Fourteen turns, and Redain is about four turns of walking from the courtyard.

**4. Thieves starting fights.** Addreorer walking up to a healthy Buroc dies **52.5% of the time** to the counterattack alone. Thieves finish. They never open.

## If it goes wrong

The win condition is three hexes from the chamber doorway and Delenia can reach it in one move. If you lose two units, or the grind stalls, or you hit turn 14 with the fight unresolved, go west and kill Redain. Even a bare win keeps 80% of your gold, adds the early-finish bonus, and carries the whole roster into scenario 03 intact.

## Things I am not sure about

- **Whether the garrison will actually come out** once the truce breaks. Nothing in the scenario tells them to hold position, but they have no villages to want either. Test it: break the truce, end the turn, run `bun run state`, and see whether anything moved. If they sit tight, the courtyard plan is dead and you either fight them in the corridor at y=9 or just take the win.
- **Whether walking through a trigger hex counts.** Assume it does.
- **Whether Redain leaves his chamber.** He cannot recruit, which sometimes frees a leader to advance and sometimes does not.

## A note on the odds tool

Two things it currently gets wrong here, so read its output with these in mind.

It ignores backstab when deciding which weapon to show you, so `--backstab` can pick the wrong one. It offered Delenia's thrown knife instead of her backstabbing sword against Lyryn and understated her by ten damage. Force the weapon with `--attack=` when it looks off.

It also does not know about Redain's forced 20% hit chance against Efraim and Delenia, so every number it gives you for his counterattack against those two is worse than reality.
