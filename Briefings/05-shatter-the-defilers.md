# 05 Shatter the Defilers

Legend of the Invincibles, Part I, NORMAL. Read off the live turn 1 board (`LotI1-Shatter the Defilers-Auto-Save1`), the scenario file and the map.

## The shape of it

A long march to a short fight. Three Orcish Warlords, and this time they are not scattered across the map like scenario 4: they sit in one corner, **four and seven movement apart from each other**. One army kills all three.

The cost is distance. They are **seventy-four movement** from your keep, which is ten to twelve turns of walking through forest, and you have thirty-seven turns. So the scenario gives you roughly twenty-four turns of fighting after a twelve-turn approach, against an enemy earning almost nothing.

Two things changed underneath you since scenario 4. **Lethalia's whole company joined**: seventeen elves are on the board, free, including an Avenger, a Hero, a Marksman, a Captain and two Rangers. And your gold collapsed to **116**, so the army you own is much bigger than the army you can deploy.

## The one number that drives every decision

**Eight.** That is the three orc sides' combined income per turn: four, two and two.

They start with 600 gold between them and earn almost nothing after that. Over the whole scenario they will add under 300 more. Compare scenario 4, where the orcs earned 62 a turn and every turn of delay cost you two more orcs.

Here delay costs you nothing and buys you everything: 47 villages, a treasury, and time to recall the twenty-one veterans currently sitting on the bench. **Take the long road and arrive rich.**

## Opening budget

116 gold is the binding constraint. Twenty-one units on the bench at 20 each is 420 gold you do not have.

- **Turn 1**: recall the four best, roughly 80 gold. **Huwyn** (Poison and a helm already on him), **Yddin** the Bandit at 44 of 61, **Arcer** the Rogue at 27, and **Deoran** the Longbowman. That leaves about 36.
- **Turns 2 onward**: take villages and recall two or three more each turn as income arrives. You do not need the whole army until turn 14 at the earliest.
- **Do not recruit.** A fresh Thug at 13 gold adds nothing you need; you have twenty-one veterans queueing for the same slots at 20, several of them two kills from levelling.

Watch upkeep. Seventeen elves plus recalls is a large fielded army, and upkeep is one per level per non-loyal unit. Your income only goes positive once you hold twenty-odd villages, which is around turn 6 to 8 on the march.

## The plan

**Turns 1 to 3, organise and start walking.** Equip everything, recall four, and move south-west. Take the six villages within twenty movement on the way past. Put someone on the keep at (30,7).

**Turns 3 to 12, the march.** This is the scenario. Move as a broad front rather than a column, take every village you pass, and recall two or three units a turn out of the growing income. Thirteen villages sit between twenty-one and forty movement out, eighteen more between forty-one and sixty. By the time you are in range of the orcs you should be holding thirty or more.

**Expect to be met on the road.** The orcs have 600 gold and nothing to spend it on but an attack. If they come to you, that is the best outcome available: you fight them in your own forest, next to your own villages, with Shamans healing, and their 600 gold burns without you having to storm three castles. Let them come.

**Turns 12 to 20, close on the pocket.** All three keeps are within seven of each other, so there is no splitting this time. Establish a line at the mouth of the corner, on forest, and grind.

**Turns 20 to 33, take the three keeps in order.** Vrong at (9,32) is nearest to the approach, then Drobrulag at (5,31) four further, then Mrud at (2,28) four beyond that. Land each assault on a daylight turn: 20, 21, 26, 27, 32, 33.

**Leave four turns of slack.** Thirty-seven is generous but the march eats a third of it.

## What will actually hurt you

**1. Your own losses.** Scenario 3 cost nine units and scenario 4 cost six. Seventeen dead across the run so far, and every one of them took their experience and their equipment with them. This scenario has no time pressure, which removes the usual excuse for trading units. Fight patiently.

**2. Lethalia, and this time she is unprotected.** In scenario 4 the orcs were forced to 10% against her. There is no such rule here: she is a 51 hit point Enchantress on your own side and a loss condition. Keep her behind the line and out of Wolf Rider reach.

**3. Wolf Riders and Goblin Knights.** Movement 8 and 7, and they will go around a broad front for whichever of your three loss conditions is least protected.

**4. Troll Shamans and Trolls.** Side 4 recruits Shamans; Trolls regenerate 8 a turn. Poison is the counter and you now have two doses of it.

**5. The march itself.** Seventy-four movement of forest at two per hex. A unit that falls behind arrives alone.

## If it goes wrong

There is no early cash-out: the objective is all three leaders. But the clock is generous, so the failure mode is losing units rather than losing the scenario. If the approach goes badly, pull back onto villages and let the orcs' 600 gold spend itself against your line, then advance again. Their income is eight a turn; they cannot rebuild and you can.

## Still unverified

- **Whether the orcs advance or garrison.** Their combined 600 gold is spent early either way, but whether it walks to you or waits in three castles decides whether this is a defensive scenario or a siege. Probe it by turn 6: advance a scout and see whether anything comes north.
- **Whether Efraim's advancement at 100 experience is an AMLA.** His unit line says it advances to nothing, so at 97 of 100 his next hit should offer an after-max-level advancement rather than a new unit type. Read the dialogue when it fires.
- **Whether the Heathenbasher fits a Bandit's mace.** The weapon is called `mace-spiked` and the item is a mace, so it should, but equip it and read the attack line before counting on 10x4.
- **What Delenia's existing advancement was.** Her maximum experience went from 113 to 136 and her hit points from 62 to 65, which is the signature of one AMLA taken during scenario 4. Check her ability list to see whether it was the Doctrine's first step.
