# 04 Paradise Lost, after action

Won. Three orc leaders killed inside twenty-five turns, at a cost of six units and with the elvish company joining permanently.

## What happened

The plan called for killing Grub Drung first because he had one movement point and sat thirteen away, then taking his keep as a central base and splitting into two columns for the other two camps. That is broadly what happened, and the reasoning behind it held: the two remaining camps really were thirty-three and thirty-four apart, and there really was no way to do them in sequence.

The cost came from the split. Two columns of nine or ten against a funded orc camp each is thin, and six of the units lost were level 1 bodies in those columns rather than anything that died to a mistake at the centre. Lethalia survived without needing rescue, exactly as the 10% forced miss rate predicted, and she and her whole company transferred to your side at the end, which is the largest single gain of the campaign so far.

## What went well

- **Grub Drung first was the right call and for the right reason.** One movement point meant he could not retreat, reinforce or reposition, and killing him early removed 22 of the enemy's 62 income per turn permanently.
- **Not marching to save Lethalia.** The briefing said the orcs were forced to 10% against her and that two reinforcement events backstopped her before turn 8. That held. Four turns of Delenia's movement were not spent on a rescue that was never needed.
- **Efraim gained 68 experience and reached level 3.** The most productive scenario any single unit has had. Putting him where he could land finishing blows rather than parking him safe was worth it.
- **Four level ups in one scenario**, including two Thugs into Bandits, which is what finally gave the Heathenbasher a home three scenarios after it dropped.

## What went badly

- **The split cost six units.** Sending two thin columns into two funded camps was the plan and the plan was probably right, but the columns were built out of level 1 bodies with 23 to 33 hit points and no healer. The lesson is not "do not split", it is **do not split without giving each column a wall**.
- **Ylla was the expensive one.** A level 2 Rogue with the loyal trait, which is a genuinely rare thing, lost with 18 experience on her. Loyal units cost no upkeep and should be treated as semi-permanent, not as line infantry.
- **The bench was still full when the columns were thin.** 371 gold on the board and only six castle hexes at the keep meant deployment took three turns, and the columns left before everyone was out.

## Where the briefing was wrong

Checked against `Briefings/04-paradise-lost.md`, prediction by prediction.

**Right.** Grub Drung's immobility, the 10% forced miss on Lethalia, the two backstop events, the guaranteed boss drops, the distances between camps, and the judgement that the objective allows no early cash-out.

**Wrong, and worth naming.**

- **"371 gold, the most you have ever started with."** That was the board figure, which is the scenario's own 200 plus 171 of carryover. The carryover itself was 171. Wesnoth adds scenario gold on top of what you carry, and the briefing collapsed the two into one number.
- **The item advice was written before checking who could use what.** The briefing said to try the Heathenbasher on a Thug because Aran was dead and no certain mace user remained. Thugs swing clubs and it did not fit; what fitted was a **Bandit**, which is what a Thug becomes at level 2. The item sat unused for the whole scenario when a level up would have unlocked it.
- **"Leave Stormrend."** Correct conclusion, but the reasoning should be recorded: `damage=-20` is minus twenty percent, and converting to lightning buys nothing against an enemy that already takes full blade damage. It stayed in the bag and then vanished from it, so it was equipped by somebody or lost with them; the diff cannot tell which.

## What carries forward

22 units, 116 gold, and **17 elves who are now yours**: an Avenger at 66 hit points, a Hero, a Marksman, a Captain, two Rangers, five Archers and three Shamans. The Shamans are the first healers this army has ever had.

Delenia took an advancement during the scenario: her maximum experience went from 113 to 136 and her hit points from 62 to 65, which is the signature of one after-max-level advancement. Efraim sits at 97 of 100 and will take his on the first hit of scenario 5.

Two units are now worth protecting the way Delenia and Efraim are: **Galithas** the Elvish Avenger, and whichever Bandit ends up carrying the Heathenbasher.
